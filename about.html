    A roastery that keeps time

    We started with one second-hand roaster, a borrowed garage and a simple idea: every hour of the day deserves the right cup.

    Our story

    Sundial began in 2019 when three friends got tired of burnt, anonymous coffee. We bought green beans directly from small farms, learned to roast them lightly and sold our first bags at a Sunday market.

    Along the way we noticed something: nobody wants the same drink at 7 AM and at 10 PM. So we built our menu around the hours of the day, and the sundial became our symbol.

    Today we roast every morning, brew in our Canal View cafe and ship fresh bags across Pakistan. The recipe has not changed: buy fairly, roast carefully, serve warmly.

    From farm to your cup

    Four steps, done the same way every day.

    1. Source

      We buy green beans from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Kenya, and visit each one every year.

    2. Roast

      Light roasts in small batches at 6 AM, tasted blind before any bag leaves the roastery.

    3. Brew

      Baristas dial in every grinder each morning, so the first cup tastes like the last.

    4. Serve

      Warm cups, real plates and a table for as long as you like.

    What we care about

    Fair sourcing

    We pay farms above market price and publish where each lot comes from.

    Fresh, always

    Nothing sits on a shelf. Beans are roasted in the morning and sold within two weeks.

    A table for everyone

    Students, freelancers, families and first dates all get the same warm welcome.

    How we got here

    1. 2019

      First roast in a borrowed garage and a stall at the Sunday market.

    2. 2021

      Opened the Canal View cafe with eight tables and one espresso machine.

    3. 2023

      Launched home delivery of fresh beans and our catering service.

    4. 2025

      Started barista workshops and a wholesale programme for local cafes.
